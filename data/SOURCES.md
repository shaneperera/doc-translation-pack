# Sources and licences

Everything in this pack was downloaded from the public internet. Nothing here is
Heidi data, customer data, or a real patient record — every document is a blank
official form or a published article.

Each was flattened to a 200 dpi raster so that it has no text layer; page selections
are noted below. `JPN_referral` was additionally put through a simulated photocopy
(greyscale, noise, JPEG). Nothing was rotated or rescaled, so the ground-truth boxes
still describe where the text is.
